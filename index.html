02About

A designer who thinks in volume.

Edward Aranda

Multidisciplinary visual designer and 3D artist with over three years of experience crafting digital advertising, visual concepts and audiovisual content.

I specialize in character modeling, digital sculpting and character animation, with a strong foundation in texturing, shading, lighting, composition and rendering. My work blends 3D production with 2D digital art, keyframe animation and motion graphics — translating creative concepts into cohesive visual experiences for brands and digital platforms.

From asset creation and scene development to final render and post-production, I like to own the whole pipeline — and obsess over the details that make an image feel real.
